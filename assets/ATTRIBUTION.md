# Map data source

Source: Alibaba Cloud DataV GeoAtlas
URL: https://geo.datav.aliyun.com/areas_v3/bound/100000_full.json
Retrieved: 2026-10-06

The source GeoJSON is retained in `china-provinces.geojson`. Province rings were projected into a regional SVG view, rounded to one decimal place, and simplified by dropping near-duplicate subpixel vertices. The visualization uses approximate city centers and illustrative curved connections; it does not depict travel routes or precise campus locations.
